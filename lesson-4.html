Lesson 4: The Verb TO BE & Spoken Fluency

No children's grammar slides today. We work directly from this interactive dashboard to drill the verb to be (am / is / are), eliminate the "it's vs is" bug, and practice speaking about real life, work, and Bali.

🗣 Denis's opening line (say this in the first 20 seconds of the call):

"Hi Eliza! Today let's work directly from my screen. I prepared our interactive classroom page with our homework and life practice so we can practice speaking without reading textbook slides. Can you see my screen?"

The #1 Mistake We Are Fixing Today
❌ My best friend it's Valery  |  ❌ Cyprus it's hot  |  ❌ Fish it's animal ✅ My best friend IS Valery  |  ✅ Cyprus IS hot  |  ✅ Fish IS an animal

The Golden Rule: it's = it is. If you already named the subject (friend, Cyprus, car, laptop), DO NOT add "it". Simply use is or are!

Block 1 · 00–05 min

Homework Blitz: Checking Rule 6 (20 Irregular Nouns)

Warm-up Drill 5 minutes

How Eliza runs this block:

Eliza says the singular noun, and Denis immediately speaks the full sentence with the plural form (without looking at the text if possible).

Eliza: "knife" → Denis: "Could you please give me two sharp knives?"
Eliza: "woman" → Denis: "Three women work on the new design project." (check: /ˈwɪm.ɪn/)
Eliza: "person" → Denis: "There are many creative people in Bali."
Eliza: "series" → Denis: "I watched two great detective series on Netflix."
Eliza: "shelf" → Denis: "I have three wooden shelves for my books and equipment."

Block 2 · 05–20 min

The Core Engine: am / is / are + Subject Pronouns

Accuracy Drill · Strict Correction 15 minutes

Read out loud without adding "it's"

Denis reads each sentence out loud. Eliza's job: if Denis says "it's", interrupt immediately and say: "Stop, say: IS".

1. My best friend is Valery, and he is in Cyprus right now.
2. My laptop is fast and reliable for complex UI design.
3. The weather in Cyprus is warm and sunny today.
4. These two developers are very talented and professional.
5. My parents are in Kazakhstan, and they are doing well.
6. Bali is an Indonesian island with a vibrant digital nomad community.
7. Our clients are from Europe and the United States.
8. My apartment is spacious, and the rooms are very bright.

Block 3 · 20–35 min

Real Life Interview: Q&A About Denis's World

Speaking & Listening 15 minutes

Eliza asks these 5 questions, Denis answers in complete sentences

Rule: Denis must not give one-word answers. Always answer with a subject and verb ("My job is...", "Bali is...").

Who is your best friend, and what is he like?
Model: "My best friend is Valery. He is a smart person, and we are often discussing our projects together."
What is your daily work as a designer?
Model: "My job is digital product design. My responsibilities are creating user interfaces, mobile apps, and brand assets."
Why is Bali attractive for your lifestyle?
Model: "Bali is attractive because the ocean is close, the lifestyle is calm, and there are many creative people."
Where are your favorite places in Cyprus?
Model: "The beaches are wonderful, and the mountain villages are very peaceful on weekends."
What is your favorite story game, and why?
Model: "My favorite game is GTA. The storylines are exciting, and the open world is full of interesting details."

Block 4 · 35–45 min

Negatives & Questions: am not / isn't / aren't

Sentence Structure 10 minutes

Eliminating "I'm not like" and "he not designer"

Practice saying correct negative sentences about life:

1. I am not a beginner in design; I am an experienced art director.
2. Cyprus isn't cheap, but it is safe and comfortable.
3. My friends aren't in Bali yet, but they are planning to visit.
4. The gym isn't far from my house; it is only five minutes away.
5. We aren't tired; we are ready to continue practicing!

Block 5 · 45–55 min

Free Speaking: "My Move to Bali & Future Routine"

Fluency Mode · Do NOT Interrupt 10 minutes

Denis speaks for 3 minutes without interruption

Instructions for Eliza: Please do not correct Denis while he speaks. Just listen and write down his mistakes on your notepad. After he finishes, give him his top 3 mistakes.

Suggested talking points for Denis:

• Why I decided to move to Bali.
• What my morning and working day will look like.
• What activities I want to try (surfing, gym, exploring the island).
• What I am looking forward to most.

Wrap-up & Homework for Lesson 5:

Eliza reviews the 3 biggest mistakes from today's class, and gives homework: 10 sentences using do / does / don't for Lesson 5.

Rules for Eliza

How to make this lesson 10x more effective

Correct every "it's" out loud immediately in Blocks 2 & 3 Say "Stop, say IS", and make Denis repeat the whole sentence correctly.
