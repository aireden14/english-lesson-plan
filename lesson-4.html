The #1 Mistake We Are Fixing Today
❌ My best friend it's Valery  |  ❌ Cyprus it's hot  |  ❌ Fish it's animal ✅ My best friend IS Valery  |  ✅ Cyprus IS hot  |  ✅ Fish IS an animal

The Golden Rule: it's = it is. If you already named the subject (friend, Cyprus, car, laptop), DO NOT add "it". Simply use is or are!

Block 3.7 · Code Review for English

Spot the Mistake: Denis Catches the Bug (10 Sentences)

Denis acts as the QA Lead or Code Reviewer. Each sentence contains a real mistake that Russian-speaking designers make. Denis must spot the bug, explain why it fails, and say the clean sentence out loud.

How Denis solves each bug card: 1. Read the buggy sentence.
2. Explain the error: "The bug here is using 'it's' when the subject is already mentioned..."
3. Say the clean, corrected sentence out loud with solid confidence!
Bug #01 Error: Double Subject ("it's" vs "is")
❌ "My MacBook it's brand new and very powerful for 3D graphics."
The Bug: You already named the subject (My MacBook). Saying "it's" adds an unnecessary pronoun it ("My MacBook it is...").
✅ Denis says: "My MacBook is brand new and very powerful for 3D graphics."
Bug #02 Error: Double Subject ("it's" vs "is")
❌ "Figma it's the industry standard tool for digital product designers."
The Bug: Figma is the proper noun subject. Drop the extra it and connect directly with is.
✅ Denis says: "Figma is the industry standard tool for digital product designers."
Bug #03 Error: Double Subject ("it's" vs "is")
❌ "The villa in Pererenan it's quiet and surrounded by green rice fields."
The Bug: The entire noun phrase "The villa in Pererenan" is the subject. Do not attach it's.
✅ Denis says: "The villa in Pererenan is quiet and surrounded by green rice fields."
Bug #04 Error: Double Subject ("it's" vs "is")
❌ "His design portfolio it's full of modern mobile applications."
The Bug: Redundant pronoun it after His design portfolio.
✅ Denis says: "His design portfolio is full of modern mobile applications."
Bug #05 Error: Double Subject ("it's" vs "is")
❌ "The weather in Canggu it's hot and tropical today."
The Bug: The weather in Canggu is the subject. Connect directly to the adjective with is.
✅ Denis says: "The weather in Canggu is hot and tropical today."
Bug #06 Error: Missing Plural -S
❌ "Denis manages three active client from California and two project this month."
The Bug: Quantifiers three and two require plural noun endings (clients, projects).
✅ Denis says: "Denis manages three active clients from California and two projects this month."
Bug #07 Error: Missing Plural -S
❌ "There are four modern coworking space and five good gym in this neighborhood."
The Bug: Plural count requires spaces and gyms.
✅ Denis says: "There are four modern coworking spaces and five good gyms in this neighborhood."
Bug #08 Error: Missing Plural -S & Subject-Verb Agreement
❌ "Many foreign expat love living in Bali because the island offer many benefit."
The Bug: Many expats requires plural -s; the island is 3rd person singular so the verb needs -s (offers); and many benefits needs plural -s.
✅ Denis says: "Many foreign expats love living in Bali because the island offers many benefits."
Bug #09 Error: Wrong Negative ("I'm not like")
❌ "I'm not like crowded tourist clubs because I'm prefer quiet beach sunsets."
The Bug: Like and prefer are active verbs, not adjectives! In English, we negate Present Simple action verbs with don't (never "I'm not like" or "I'm prefer").
✅ Denis says: "I don't like crowded tourist clubs because I prefer quiet beach sunsets."
Bug #10 Error: Wrong Negative ("he not have" + pronoun duplication)
❌ "My backend developer he not have the API credentials for the production server."
The Bug: Drop the extra he, and negate 3rd person singular action verbs with doesn't have (never "not have").
✅ Denis says: "My backend developer doesn't have the API credentials for the production server."
