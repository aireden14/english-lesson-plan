What I would like us to work on next

A detailed plan for our next class, and the direction I would like our lessons to take after that, so you know what to prepare.

Hi Eliza! Thank you for the last lesson — the pronunciation review at the beginning was really useful, and I liked the Understand Your Heroes and Use Me activities a lot. I wrote this page with a translator so that I could explain myself clearly.

I want to ask you to change the direction of our lessons a little.

In our first lesson I asked for an individual approach and told you that I play games, so we started learning gaming vocabulary. That was my fault — I gave you the wrong instruction. As you saw last time, I already knew all ten words and scored 10 out of 10, so that part does not teach me anything new. Please do not use Dota vocabulary anymore.

My real problem is not vocabulary. My real problem is that I do not know the basic structure of English. I do not know when to say is, when to say it's, when to say do, will, they. I know many words, but I cannot put them together correctly.

My goal is to speak with real people in real life — in shops, at work, when I travel. So I would like our lessons to be about basic English grammar, and I would like the practice sentences to be about my own life, not about games.

The target for our next lesson
My best friend it's Valery
My best friend is Valery

I say it's instead of is about 31 times in one lesson. I also asked you about this directly in lesson 2 — "Is, yes, is — or it's?" — so it is the thing that confuses me most.

I / you / he / she / it / we / they, and when to use each one. You give me a sentence with a name and I replace the name with a pronoun:
